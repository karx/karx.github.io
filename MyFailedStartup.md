# MyFailedStartup

[Project Link ↗](./myfailedstartup.com/index.html)

"We’ve all been lauded for our success, now it’s time to celebrate failures!"

MyFailedStartup was an experiment in normalizing the conversation around professional setbacks. In a culture obsessed with 'scaling' and 'exits', we wanted to create a space to archive the lessons learned from the startups that didn't make it.

## Key Themes
- Normalizing failure as a prerequisite for innovation.
- Archiving the "un-lauded" history of the startup ecosystem.
- Community building through shared vulnerability.
