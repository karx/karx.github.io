## What I'm thinking about

**Interface Patterns in the age of ubiquitous compute**

When compute is everywhere and nearly free, what happens to the conventions we built around scarcity? Interfaces designed for "desktop first" or "mobile first" feel like artifacts of a different era. The next interaction patterns might not look like apps at all — and I want to explore what they do look like.
