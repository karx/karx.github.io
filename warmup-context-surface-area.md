# Warmup Post — Context as Surface Area

Warmup thought — putting together a longer post on what agents are teaching us. This is the idea I keep coming back to.

---

Spent a lot of time recently listening to how builders talk about agents.
The word that comes up most: context.

Usually as something you *give*.

I think it's something you *grow*.

Every loop, every action, every decision — the agent expands the surface area of what it knows, what it can touch, what it can act on. Context isn't the water it swims in. It's the ground it builds beneath itself.

The better the agent, the more it grows.

The richer the context it leaves behind — the further the next loop reaches.

---

This one idea changed how I think about building with AI.

Not "what context should I give it?"

But "what kind of context is this agent capable of creating?"
