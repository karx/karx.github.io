# Member Charter

## Who Can Join

Any individual who resides or regularly works within the locality boundary may become a Member.

There are no fees to join. There is no approval committee. Membership begins when a person completes onboarding and is confirmed by one existing Member.

---

## Member Profile

Every Member maintains a profile with:

- **Locality** — verified residence or workplace within the boundary
- **Skills** — self-declared, then verified over time through completed work
- **Time availability** — hours per week available for work
- **Reputation score** — built from completed jobs, peer confirmations, and community contributions
- **Contribution weight** — cumulative measure of community value created (see Governance Charter)

---

## Member Rights

- Post work items to the marketplace
- Claim work items matching their skill level
- Earn SilkCoins for completed, verified work
- Request cash-out of earned SilkCoins (see Token Charter)
- Propose and vote on Shared Fund allocations (weight-gated — see Governance Charter)
- Access SilkRoute skill modules to grow their profile

---

## Member Responsibilities

- Complete claimed work to the standard described in the job
- Submit honest proof of completion
- Confirm or reject peer completion claims fairly
- Uphold the community's founding principles

---

## Skill Levels

Skills progress through three stages:

| Stage | How achieved |
|---|---|
| **Declared** | Member self-reports the skill at onboarding |
| **Peer-verified** | Two Members with the same skill confirm competency |
| **Earned** | Member has completed 3+ jobs requiring this skill |

Jobs specify the minimum skill stage required to claim them.

---

## Member Standing

A Member in good standing has:
- No unresolved dispute outcomes against them
- Completed at least 50% of claimed jobs in the last 90 days

A Member not in good standing may post jobs but cannot claim new ones until standing is restored.

---

## Exit

A Member may leave at any time. Earned SilkCoins remain claimable for 90 days after exit. Contribution weight is preserved in the ledger but becomes inactive.
