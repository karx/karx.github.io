Pilot · Belandur, Outer Ring Road · Bengaluru

SilkRoute — work your neighbourhood runs on.

Your OMR complex already runs on informal favours — parking help, wiring fixes, CCTV checks. SilkRoute makes that formal, fair, and visible.

How it works

  • Post a job — or claim one. Wiring, parking rosters, common-area upkeep, CCTV checks.
  • Prove completion with a photo or note. Two neighbours confirm.
  • Get paid in SilkCoins — 1 SC = ₹10, fixed.
  • 15% of every job funds community roles: parking attendants, traffic coordinators — chosen by the community, not a company.

Why this is different

  • No platform takes a cut. The community is the platform.
  • Governance weight comes from contribution — posting jobs, completing them, confirming peers. No elections, no inherited authority.
  • Vendors are capped at 10% of community voice, so they can't capture it.
  • Cash-out is simple: 72 hours, minimum 10 SC, at the fixed rate.

Phase 1 — Belandur

One locality. Five founding members. Three seed jobs to start: parking roster coordination, common-area wiring checks, CCTV inspection. Real transactions, a seeded shared fund, and a transparent ledger anyone can read.
