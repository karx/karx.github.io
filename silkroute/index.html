SilkRoute · Pilot

A work economy for Belandur, Outer Ring Road

Your complex already runs on informal favours — parking help, wiring fixes, CCTV checks. SilkRoute makes that formal, fair, and visible.

What it is

SilkRoute is a hyperlocal work economy for one neighbourhood. You post a job, a neighbour claims it, proves it, and gets paid in SilkCoins — at a fixed rate of 1 SC = ₹10. Fifteen percent of every job funds community roles: parking attendants, traffic coordinators, chosen by the community, not a company.

No platform cut. There is no company taking a fee. The community is the platform.

How a job works

  1. Someone posts a job — wiring, parking roster, CCTV check, anything local.
  2. A neighbour with the right skill claims it.
  3. They submit proof (photo or text).
  4. Two of three neighbours confirm it was done.
  5. SilkCoins are released. 15% routes automatically to the Shared Fund.

Why it works here

  • Density — apartment clusters along OMR mean neighbours are close enough to actually verify work.
  • Skills — tech workers with spare time for networking, electronics, coordination.
  • Pain — parking chaos, traffic, common-area upkeep. These are daily, not occasional.

Governance, by contribution

Your voice in community decisions grows with what you do — completing jobs, posting jobs, confirming work. No elections, no inherited authority. Vendors are capped at 10% of total community weight so they can't capture the process.
