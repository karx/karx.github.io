# Governance Charter

## The Principle

Governance is not designed in. It emerges from use.

The more a Member enables work — posting jobs, completing jobs, contributing to the community — the more weight their voice carries when the community makes decisions. No elections. No committees. No inherited authority.

---

## Contribution Weight

Every Member accumulates **Contribution Weight (CW)** over time.

| Action | CW earned |
|---|---|
| Job completed and verified | 2 CW |
| Job posted and successfully completed by another | 1 CW |
| Peer confirmation provided | 0.5 CW |
| Dispute resolved fairly (confirmed by outcome) | 1 CW |
| Skill module completed | 0.5 CW |

CW does not decay. It is a permanent record of community contribution.

---

## Proposals

Any Member with **20+ CW** may raise a Shared Fund proposal.

A proposal must specify:
- What the fund will be spent on (Community Role, infrastructure job, or skill module)
- How much SC / ₹ is required
- Duration (one-off or recurring, and for how long)
- Success criteria — how the community will know it worked

---

## Voting

Voting is open for **72 hours** after a proposal is raised.

- Eligible voters: all Members with 5+ CW
- Vote weight: proportional to CW at time of vote
- Quorum: votes cast must represent at least 20% of total community CW
- Pass threshold: >60% weighted yes

If quorum is not reached, the proposal lapses and may be re-raised after 7 days.

---

## Vendor Governance Cap

Vendor Members may accumulate CW through posting and enabling jobs.  
Vendor CW is capped at **10% of total community CW** at any time.  
This prevents vendor capture of the governance process.

---

## What Governance Can Change

Through a passed proposal, the community may:
- Fund a Community Role or infrastructure job
- Adjust the Shared Fund contribution rate
- Adjust the reserve floor
- Amend any charter parameter recorded in `local.md`
- Deprecate a Community Role

Governance cannot change the founding principles in the Community Charter.

---

## Transparency

All proposals, votes, and outcomes are recorded in the community ledger and visible to all Members.
