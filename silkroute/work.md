# Work Charter

## Job Types

| Type | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **One-off** | Discrete task, done once | WiFi setup, CCTV repair, painting a wall |
| **Recurring** | Repeated on a schedule | Weekly parking coordination, monthly drain clearing |
| **Community Role** | Ongoing funded position, approved by governance | Traffic management, common area attendant |

---

## Posting a Job

Any Member may post a job. A job posting must include:

- **What** — clear description of work required
- **Skill required** — minimum skill level (Declared / Peer-verified / Earned)
- **Time estimate** — expected hours
- **Token reward** — SilkCoins offered (minimum: 1 SilkCoin per hour)
- **Deadline** — when the work must be completed by
- **Proof requirement** — what counts as evidence of completion (photo, confirmation by recipient, etc.)

A posting is active once published. It remains open until claimed or expired.

---

## Claiming a Job

A Member may claim a job if:
- Their skill level meets the requirement
- They have the stated time available
- They are in good standing

Only one Member may hold a claim at a time. If a claim is abandoned (no activity for 48 hours), the job returns to open.

---

## Completing a Job

Upon completion, the Member submits:
- Proof as specified in the posting
- A brief note on what was done

---

## Verification

Completion is verified by **2 of 3 confirmation**:

1. The job poster confirms receipt
2. One other Member who can observe the work confirms
3. (If 1 and 2 are the same person, a second independent Member confirms)

Once verified, SilkCoins are released to the worker. The Shared Fund contribution is deducted automatically (see Token Charter).

---

## Disputes

A dispute is raised when the poster rejects a completion claim.

**Resolution process:**
1. Worker and poster each submit their account (written, in the job thread)
2. Three randomly selected Members with relevant skill review and vote
3. Majority decision is final
4. False dispute raised by poster → poster loses 2 reputation points
5. False completion claim by worker → worker loses 5 reputation points and job is re-opened

---

## Community Roles

Community Roles are funded by the Shared Fund and approved through governance. They follow the same claim and verification process but:

- The "poster" is the community (represented by the Shared Fund)
- Recurring shifts are auto-posted on schedule
- The worker for a Community Role may hold the position for up to 90 days before an open re-bid
