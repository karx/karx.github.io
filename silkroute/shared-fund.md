# Shared Fund Charter

## What It Is

The Shared Fund is the community's collective resource. It is not owned by any individual or vendor. It exists to fund work the community cannot assign to a single person's initiative — the parking attendant, the wiring crew, the traffic coordinator.

---

## How It Is Fed

| Source | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Job completion contribution | X% of every job reward, auto-deducted and routed (default: 15%) |
| RWA allocation | One-time or periodic transfer from RWA maintenance budget |
| Vendor contribution | Local businesses contribute directly to the ₹ reserve |
| Open contributions | Any individual or organisation may donate |

The default contribution rate (15%) is set at locality founding and recorded in `local.md`. It can be changed only through a governance proposal.

---

## What It Funds

The Shared Fund can only be spent in three ways:

1. **Community Roles** — salaries for approved ongoing positions
2. **Community Infrastructure Jobs** — one-off or recurring work items posted on behalf of the whole community
3. **Skill Module development** — commissioning new upskilling content relevant to the locality

It cannot be used to reimburse individual Members for personal expenses, pay external vendors not registered with SilkRoute, or fund anything outside a passed governance proposal.

---

## Transparency

- Current ₹ balance: visible to all Members at all times
- Incoming and outgoing transactions: logged in the community ledger
- Cash-out queue: visible to all Members
- Approved proposals and their draw-down: visible to all Members

---

## Reserve Floor

The fund maintains a **reserve floor** — a minimum ₹ balance that cannot be spent. This ensures cash-out requests can always be honoured.

- Default reserve floor: ₹5,000 (set at founding, recorded in `local.md`)
- If the fund drops below floor, Community Role payouts pause until replenished
- Cash-out requests are never blocked by this floor — they are the priority

---

## Seeding

At founding, the Shared Fund is seeded by:
- RWA partnership contribution
- Local vendor contributions
- Open fund (public/crowdfunded)

Seeding amounts and contributors are recorded permanently in `local.md` as founding acknowledgements.
