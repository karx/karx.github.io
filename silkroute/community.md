# Community Charter

## What SilkRoute Is

SilkRoute is a hyperlocal economy. It exists at the scale of a neighbourhood — small enough that members know each other, large enough to sustain shared roles.

It is not a company. It is not an app. It is an agreement between neighbours about how to exchange skill, time, and value — and how to use that exchange to fund the things the neighbourhood needs.

---

## Founding Principles

**1. Value stays local**  
Every transaction on SilkRoute generates a contribution to the community's Shared Fund. No external platform extracts a cut.

**2. Skill is democratic**  
Anyone can learn. Anyone can contribute. The mobile device has collapsed the skill gap — SilkRoute's upskilling modules exist to close it further.

**3. Work is dignified**  
Every job posted has a fair token reward. Claiming and completing work is an act of community service, not charity.

**4. Governance is earned, not assigned**  
No elections. No committees by default. Influence over the community fund grows from contribution — jobs enabled and jobs completed.

**5. The system must sustain itself**  
Seed funding starts the engine. After that, the community's own activity must keep it running. Any charter that requires permanent external funding is a failed charter.

---

## What SilkRoute Is Not

- It is not a charity. Members earn real value for real work.
- It is not a corporation. There are no shareholders, no profit motive.
- It is not a DAO. Governance is grounded in physical, local relationships.
- It is not a replacement for the RWA. It complements existing structures.

---

## Locality

Each SilkRoute instance is bound to a specific locality. The locality is defined at founding and recorded in `local.md`. Members must reside or work within the locality boundary.
