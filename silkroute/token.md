# Token Charter

## The SilkCoin

The SilkCoin (SC) is the unit of exchange within SilkRoute.

- **1 SC = ₹10** (fixed rate, set at locality founding, recorded in `local.md`)
- SilkCoins are not speculative. They do not fluctuate.
- The Shared Fund holds real ₹. SilkCoins are the interface.

---

## Earning SilkCoins

SilkCoins are earned by completing verified work.

Upon job verification:
- Worker receives the job's stated SC reward
- A community contribution is automatically deducted from the reward and routed to the Shared Fund (see Shared Fund Charter for %)

There is no other way to manufacture SilkCoins. They come from work.

---

## Spending SilkCoins

| Use | Cost |
|---|---|
| Post a job | Free (SilkCoins used only as reward, not as posting fee) |
| Access paid skill modules | As priced per module |
| Tip another Member | Voluntary, any amount |
| Cash out | See below |

---

## Cashing Out

Any Member may request a cash-out of their SilkCoin balance.

**Process:**
1. Member submits cash-out request with amount and UPI/bank details
2. Request is fulfilled within 72 hours from the Shared Fund's ₹ reserve
3. Equivalent SC are burned from the Member's balance
4. Minimum cash-out: 10 SC (₹100)

**Important:** Cash-out is fulfilled from the ₹ reserve. If the reserve is low, cash-out requests queue and are fulfilled as the fund replenishes. Members can see the current reserve and queue at any time — full transparency.

---

## Ledger

Every SC transaction is recorded in the community ledger:
- Earned (job ID, worker, amount)
- Shared Fund deduction (job ID, amount)
- Spent (type, amount)
- Cashed out (member, amount, date)

The ledger is readable by all Members. It is the source of truth.

---

## Token Integrity

- SilkCoins cannot be created outside of verified work completion
- SilkCoins cannot be transferred peer-to-peer in Phase 1 (prevents gaming)
- Vendor contributions to the Shared Fund do not create SilkCoins — they replenish the ₹ reserve directly
