# Vendor Charter

## Who Is a Vendor

A Vendor is a local business — shop, service provider, workshop — that operates within or near the locality boundary and chooses to participate in SilkRoute.

Vendors are not separate from the community. They are members of it, with the same rights and responsibilities as individual Members — plus specific terms that prevent their scale from distorting the community.

---

## Joining

A Vendor joins by:
1. Being nominated by an existing Member
2. Contributing a minimum amount to the Shared Fund (recorded in `local.md`)
3. Being confirmed by Members with combined CW ≥ 50

---

## What Vendors Can Do

- Post jobs to the marketplace (e.g. a local hardware shop needing delivery help)
- Access the verified talent pool to find skilled local Members for their needs
- Contribute to the Shared Fund at any time
- Earn CW through posting and enabling work (subject to cap — see Governance Charter)

---

## What Vendors Get

- **Visibility** — listed in the community directory as a SilkRoute Vendor
- **Talent access** — first visibility of skilled Members open to work
- **Community trust** — association with a transparent, contribution-based community

---

## Vendor Obligations

- Pay job rewards in SilkCoins (converted from ₹ at the standard rate)
- Abide by the Work Charter in all job postings
- Not post jobs that compete with Community Roles already funded by the Shared Fund

---

## Anti-Capture Rules

- Vendor CW capped at 10% of total community CW (see Governance Charter)
- A Vendor may not hold more than one proposal open at a time
- Vendor contributions to the Shared Fund do not purchase governance weight directly — only completed work earns CW
